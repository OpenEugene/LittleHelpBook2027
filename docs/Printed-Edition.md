# Printed Edition

The Little Help Book is also published as a printed directory of community resources for Lane County, Oregon. The most recent edition here was produced in December 2021.

- [Little Help Book, December 2021 (PDF)](.attachments/Little-Help-Book-2021.pdf)

## What is in it

- **Emergency crisis lines** on the opening pages, for people who need help right away.
- **How to use the book**, explaining where to start in a crisis and how to find help by area of need.
- **Listings by category**, each grouping providers with their contact details, hours and what they offer.
- **Community sponsors** whose contributions paid for the publication, and blank pages for notes.

## How it relates to the application

The application holds the same kind of information as the book: providers, how to reach them, and the categories they appear under. Like the book, it uses only two levels of grouping, categories and the subcategories within them. The printed edition is the reference for how listings should read and be organized.
