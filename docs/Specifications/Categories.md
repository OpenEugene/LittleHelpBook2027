# Categories

Categories group providers so people can find the kind of help they need.

## Two levels only

There are only ever two levels: categories, and subcategories within a category. For example, a Food category might hold Food Pantries and Free Meals.

## Managing categories

- People with edit permission can add, rename and delete categories and subcategories, and move a subcategory to a different category.
- Moving a subcategory keeps its providers consistent: they are also placed in the new category.
- Deleting a subcategory removes providers from it but leaves them in its category.
- Deleting a category also deletes its subcategories and removes every provider from them.
